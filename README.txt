SARVANI WEBSITE - HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE
1. Open your hosting panel (cPanel / Hostinger / GoDaddy) > File Manager > public_html.
2. Upload this zip and Extract it there, so index.html sits directly in public_html.
3. Your hosting must support PHP 7.4 or newer (almost all do). Make sure the "data" and "uploads" folders are writable (permission 755, or 775 if saving fails).
4. Open your domain, click "Admin" (top right), sign in, then FIRST go to the "ID & Password" tab and change the password.
5. Add products in the Products tab. Price, sizes and photos are managed only there.
Backup: download the "data" and "uploads" folders now and then - they hold all your products and photos.
Do not delete the .htaccess files; they protect your data.
